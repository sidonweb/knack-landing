    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is Knack free?

    Yes. No ads, no subscription, no premium tier.

    Why an APK instead of the Play Store?

    Knack is new and shared with friends first. Installing the APK takes about a minute (see the steps above), and new versions are posted on GitHub.

    Is there an iPhone version?

    Not yet. Knack is Android-only for now.

    How is the daily score worked out?

    It’s the share of what you planned that got done, weighted by priority: High counts 4, Medium 3, None 2 and Low 1. Habits count too. A score of 60 or more keeps your streak, and 100 is a perfect day. Carrying a task to tomorrow never raises today’s score.

    What happens if I miss a day?

    A day under 60 ends the streak, so plan ahead. You can mark up to two rest days a week, up to two weeks in advance, and they bridge your streak instead of breaking it.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Tasks, habits and check-ins save on your phone first and sync when you’re back online. Your score updates instantly either way.

    Who can see my progress?

    Your name, photo, level and streak are visible so friends can find you. Make your account private to approve followers, turn off Share activity to keep your days out of feeds, or hide any single post.

    Can I delete my account?

    Anytime. Go to Settings, then Delete account, and everything is removed straight away.
